## Governance

**AI may argue; only humans merge.** Contributors propose changes on branches; reviewers check internal consistency, counterarguments and cross-ADR governance; maintainers retain final merge authority. AI review is a process discipline, not a substitute for required CI or human approval. Read the [architecture decisions](https://github.com/openkubes/openkubes/tree/main/architecture/decisions) and [engineering method](https://github.com/openkubes/openkubes/blob/main/docs/platform-engineering-method.md).

## Contribute

1. Read the engineering method and relevant ADRs.
2. Ground new abstractions in real consumers, not speculative requirements.
3. Open a scoped branch and pull request, not a direct change to main.
4. Participate in review and run the actual repository checks.
5. Leave merge and publication decisions to authorized humans.

## Open research and engineering opportunities

Historical work areas include constrained edge profiles (OS, GitOps, images, storage and autonomy under partition), CLI design, requirements inventory and cluster deregistration. These are not assertions that the original spikes are still open; check the [current issues](https://github.com/openkubes/openkubes/issues) and [research overview](/research/) before starting work.
